  1. Concept first

    We start from the idea behind the topic, with a diagram, before any formula.

  2. Worked examples

    I solve one problem step by step, then you solve the next while I guide.

  3. Exam practice

    Past-paper and exam-style questions, with marking the way examiners mark.

  4. Notes and homework

    Short notes after each lesson and a few questions to try before the next one.
