# shaktikr.github.io

Source of [shaktikr.github.io](https://shaktikr.github.io): engineering services (FEA, CAD, MATLAB and Python, mechanical and aerospace calculations, computational materials, research support, tutoring) by Shakti Kumar, plus free engineering calculators and learning pages.

Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Shared styles and scripts are in `assets/`. Contact details and the optional form endpoint are in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `assets/site.js`.

Portfolio pages for hiring managers are in `portfolio/` (tutoring, AI training and computational materials, data science), with downloadable CVs in `assets/cv/`.
