Frequently asked questions

How is the price decided?

By the size of the job, not by the hour. After reading your brief I send a fixed price, a timeline and a list of what you'll receive. The price only changes if you change the scope, and I tell you before doing any extra work.

How do I pay?

International clients pay by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer in USD, EUR or GBP. Clients in India pay by UPI or bank transfer in INR. Projects are paid 50% at the start and 50% on delivery.

Will my files and data stay confidential?

Yes. I sign your NDA before you share anything, I don't publish or reuse client work, and the projects shown on this site are my own research.

I'm not in India. Can we still work together?

Yes. Almost all work is done remotely by email, WhatsApp and video call. I'm on Indian time (UTC+5:30), which overlaps with mornings in Europe and evenings in the Americas.

Which software do you use?

Open-source tools by default (Python, CalculiX, FEniCS, FreeCAD, LAMMPS, Quantum ESPRESSO). If you need Abaqus, ANSYS, COMSOL or SolidWorks files, we agree in the quote how the licence is covered.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Every price includes one round of revisions. If something in the agreed scope is wrong, I fix it at no extra cost.

Do you offer lower prices for students?

Tutoring is priced for students at $20 an hour. For graded coursework I teach the method rather than complete the work.

Can you do my assignment, exam or thesis?

No. I don't complete graded work for submission. I'm happy to teach the method, work through similar examples and review work you've done yourself.
