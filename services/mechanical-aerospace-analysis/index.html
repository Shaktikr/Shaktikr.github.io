Mechanical and aerospace engineering calculations

Clear, checked engineering calculations for design reviews, student and research projects and technical reports, from hand calculations to numerical models.

What I can help with

  • Beams, shafts, columns, springs and pressure vessels
  • Bolted and welded joint calculations
  • Composite laminates using classical lamination theory, with failure criteria
  • Fatigue life (S–N curves, Goodman) and fracture mechanics (Paris law, K_IC)
  • Sizing of lightweight and lattice structures
  • Material selection for weight, stiffness or temperature

Questions

Which methods do you use?

Standard textbook methods (Shigley, Roark, Hibbeler and similar) and published material data. If your project must follow a specific design code, send it with the brief and I'll confirm whether I can apply it.

Can you sign off or certify a design?

No. I'm not a licensed professional engineer, so I don't stamp drawings or certify designs. My reports support your engineers or the certifying engineer on your project.
