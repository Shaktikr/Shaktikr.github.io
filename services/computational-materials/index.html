Computational materials science: DFT, molecular dynamics and more

Atomistic and mesoscale simulations of metals, alloys and ceramics, carried through to the properties engineers and researchers use. This is my own research area.

What I can help with

  • DFT: lattice parameters, elastic constants and formation energies
  • DFT: vacancy, stacking-fault and surface energies
  • Molecular dynamics: deformation, diffusion, creep and grain boundaries
  • Phase-field models of sintering and microstructure evolution
  • CALPHAD phase diagrams with pycalphad
  • Creep and mechanical test data: stress exponent, activation energy, threshold stress
  • Machine learning on materials data

Questions

Can you match my experimental data?

I compare results with your data and with published values, and explain any differences. I don't tune results to fit.

How large can the simulations be?

Typical DFT jobs use up to a few hundred atoms and MD jobs up to a few million. Very large jobs may need cloud computing time, which I quote separately.
